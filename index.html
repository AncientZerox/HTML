Gas Leak Detector

About Our Gas Leak Detector

Our sensor module uses an MQ-2 gas sensor and Arduino Nano to detect flammable gases with high accuracy and trigger alarms in under 2 seconds.

Sensor

MQ-2 (Gas / Smoke)

Controller

Arduino Nano

Power

5V DC

Detection Range

300-10,000-ppm

1

Sensor Senses Gas

MQ-2 varies its resistance based on gas concentration.

2

Arduino Reads Signal

Analog voltage is converted into a digital ppm value.

3

Alarm & Display

LED indicator and buzzer alert users above threshold.
